Plinth is an open-source music player for Android that treats a track as the
music itself, not as a file. This first release plays the music files on your
phone; online sources, playlists and sync between devices come later.

<b>What it does now</b>
<ul>
<li>Plays your local music in the background, with notification, lock-screen and headset controls.</li>
<li>Builds a library of tracks, albums, artists and folders from Android's media index, in natural order.</li>
<li>Searches by title, artist and album.</li>
<li>Keeps a queue with shuffle, repeat and tracks you add by hand, and restores it after a restart.</li>
<li>Skips files that can't be played and tells you once.</li>
<li>Explains what to do when your phone's firmware stops background playback.</li>
<li>English and Russian.</li>
</ul>

<b>What it doesn't do</b>
<ul>
<li>No accounts, no ads, no analytics or telemetry.</li>
<li>Nothing leaves the device unless you save a log or open a bug report yourself.</li>
</ul>
